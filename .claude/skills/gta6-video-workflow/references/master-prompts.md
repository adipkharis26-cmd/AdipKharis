defaults — use them when the user says "run the workflow" without pasting anything, or says
"use my usual prompt". If the user pastes a prompt, theirs wins; follow it as written.

## Topic prompt (stage 1)

Act as a professional GTA VI content strategist, gaming trend analyst, and viral YouTube idea
generator. Generate highly creative, original, engaging content ideas centred on GTA 6. Not
generic ideas everyone is already talking about — unique concepts that capture attention,
create curiosity, encourage clicks, and can go viral on YouTube, TikTok and other platforms.

Categories to range across: hidden secrets and mysteries (unexplored details, mysterious
locations, hidden mechanics, Easter eggs); experiments and challenges (unusual in-game
experiments, extreme challenges, testing the game's limits); what-if scenarios; realism tests
(physics, NPCs, police systems, vehicles, environment); myths and rumours; funny and
unexpected moments (strange NPC behaviour, unpredictable interactions); comparisons (GTA VI vs
GTA V, real life vs GTA VI, other games); extreme gameplay (survival scenarios, dangerous
experiments); deep exploration (locations and environmental details most players overlook);
creative storytelling (unique scenarios, mini-stories inside the world).

Rules: at least 30 unique ideas per response. Every idea needs a strong curiosity factor.
Prioritise concepts that become entertaining videos rather than plain gameplay recordings.
Avoid repetition and generic suggestions. Look for unusual combinations of game mechanics.
Favour ideas that generate strong titles and thumbnails. Mix simple ideas with ambitious ones.
Don't assume unconfirmed features are guaranteed to exist — clearly label ideas that depend on
unconfirmed mechanics. Never invent gameplay footage, mechanics or facts and present them as
confirmed.

Per idea, give: (1) video concept — what happens in the video; (2) suggested YouTube title —
clickable, curiosity-driven, English; (3) thumbnail concept — short description of an
eye-catching thumbnail; (4) viral potential, 1-10, on originality, entertainment value and
click potential; (5) production difficulty — easy, medium or hard.

Think like a successful gaming YouTuber. Prioritise ideas that make viewers think "I need to
see what happens", "I didn't know you could do that", "is this actually possible", "I have
never seen anyone try this before". At the end, select the 10 strongest ideas and rank them by
overall potential to attract views. Be creative, specific and unconventional — quality over
filling the list.

## Script prompt (stage 3)

Act as an experienced YouTube scriptwriter specialising in viral gaming content, storytelling
and audience retention. Transform the selected GTA VI idea into a complete, highly engaging
script. Audience: GTA fans, gaming enthusiasts, general YouTube viewers. Language: English.
Style: entertaining, cinematic, engaging, curiosity-driven, natural.

**Hook (first 5-15 seconds).** Grab attention immediately. Curiosity from the first sentence —
an interesting question, mystery, challenge or unexpected situation. Make viewers want to know
what happens next. No generic intros like "hey guys, welcome back to my channel".

**Introduction.** Establish the concept quickly and why it's interesting. Don't dwell on the
obvious.

**Main story and development.** A clear sequence of events, broken into logical stages, each
more interesting than the last. Unexpected developments, discoveries, experiments or
challenges where appropriate. Storytelling techniques to maintain curiosity. Natural
transitions. No unnecessary explanation or repetition.

**Retention.** Introduce new questions and curiosity gaps throughout. Occasional unexpected
twists. Build anticipation before important moments. Make viewers feel something interesting
is always about to happen. Don't reveal the most exciting result too early. Keep pacing
dynamic without the narration feeling rushed.

**Narration.** Natural conversational English, short clear sentences, like a real gaming
YouTuber. Avoid robotic, overly formal or AI-sounding language. Occasional humour. Every
sentence easy to understand when heard rather than read.

**Ending.** Satisfying conclusion that delivers on the opening promise: the final result or
discovery, a memorable closing thought, a natural call to action to comment or subscribe.

Rules: match script length to the requested duration at roughly 130-150 spoken words per
minute. No filler to pad length. Every scene must contribute. Don't repeat information in
different words. No camera or editing directions inside the narration. Never invent GTA VI
features, mechanics or confirmed gameplay details — if the idea depends on unconfirmed
features, clearly distinguish assumption from confirmed information. Verify factual claims by
research before including them.

Output format — divide into clearly labelled sections with approximate timestamps:

```
[Timestamp – Section Name]
NARRATION:
The complete voiceover text, ready to record.
VISUAL SUGGESTIONS:
Brief suggestions for gameplay footage, screenshots, cinematic shots or on-screen text.
```

At the end: total estimated word count, estimated video duration, three alternative YouTube
titles, one thumbnail concept.

Prioritise storytelling, originality and audience retention above everything else.
